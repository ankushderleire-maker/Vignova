The job search process has evolved dramatically over the past few years. Gone are the days of sending the exact same resume to fifty different companies and hoping for a callback. Today almost every mid-size and large employer runs applications through an **Applicant Tracking System (ATS)** — and while the ATS rarely auto-rejects anyone, it does decide which resumes a recruiter sees first when a posting attracts hundreds of applicants.

But there's good news: The same AI technology used to screen you out can now be used to get you hired.
