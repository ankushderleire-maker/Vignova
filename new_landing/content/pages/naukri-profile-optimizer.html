What recruiters see

The Naukri sections that decide whether you are found

A Naukri profile is not a resume. Recruiters search it by keywords and narrow results by details such as experience and location, so each section has a job to do.

Naukri profile sections, the limits Vignova checks and what makes them work
SectionLimit Vignova checksWhat makes it work
Resume headline250 charactersYour role or target role, two to four core skills and your domain. Not a list of personal qualities.
Key skills50 skillsThe skills you want to be found for, spelled the way postings spell them, and consistent with your employment and projects.
Profile summary1,000 charactersWho you are professionally, your strongest evidence and the roles you are looking for.

How it works

From your Naukri profile to copy-ready rewrites

Naukri has no public way for other services to read your profile, so the Vignova Chrome extension reads it from the page, in your own browser, when you ask it to.

  1. Install the extension and sign in

    Add the Vignova extension to Chrome and sign in with your Vignova account.

  2. Start a scan

    Open the Naukri Optimizer in Vignova and choose Scan Naukri profile. Your Naukri profile opens in a new tab.

  3. Let the extension read it

    Sign in to Naukri in that tab if asked. The extension reads each profile section and takes you back to the results.

  4. Read the scores

    See the overall score, seven area scores, the sections that are incomplete and what to fix first. Scanning and scoring are free.

  5. Generate rewrites

    Get a rewritten resume headline, profile summary, key skills, job profiles and project details. A rewritten section is kept only if it does not lower your profile score.

  6. Copy and paste into Naukri

    Compare your current profile with the optimized one, copy each section and paste it into Naukri's edit forms.

What completeness checks

Completeness is weighted by how much recruiters rely on each Naukri section:

  • Employment added (20 points)
  • Resume headline (15), at least five key skills (15), and job profile descriptions of 15 words or more (15)
  • Education (10) and a profile summary (10)
  • IT skills, projects and career profile (5 each)

What the headline check looks for

  • At least 60 characters, and no more than the 250-character limit
  • At least three of your key skills named
  • Your role and strengths first, not availability phrases such as "looking for a job" or "fresher"

Practical advice

Getting a Naukri profile right

If you are a fresher

Lead the headline with your degree, graduation year and target role. Fill key skills from your coursework, projects and internships, add every relevant project with details, and include your CGPA or percentage in education.

If you are experienced

Naukri already shows your total experience, so use the headline for your specialism and strongest skills. Rewrite job profiles to show results, and remove skills you have not used in years.

Keep the details accurate

Recruiters can narrow searches by details such as notice period and location. An outdated notice period or city can keep you out of searches you would qualify for.

Match your resume

Employers, titles, dates and skills should agree with the resume you upload. Build both from one Master Profile so they stay consistent.

Use the words postings use

Scan a few postings for roles you want with the free resume keyword scanner, and add the skills you genuinely have to your key skills.

Review it regularly

Your profile shows when it was last updated. While you are job hunting, review it every few weeks and update it whenever something changes.

Privacy and independence

  • It does not read your phone number, email address, salary or personal details such as date of birth
  • Vignova never asks for your Naukri password
  • It never edits your Naukri profile; you paste the changes you choose

Use it for your own profile. The optimizer helps you improve the text you publish on your own Naukri profile. It does not search, message or apply on Naukri for you.
