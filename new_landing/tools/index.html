FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Which Vignova tools are free?

The resume keyword scanner is free with no sign-up. With a free account you can use the ATS resume checker and the job application tracker, score your LinkedIn and Naukri profiles, and use the Chrome extension to save jobs, check keyword matches and copy your profile details. The free plan also includes 3 introductory credits for AI actions such as a tailored resume or a cover letter.

What are credits?

AI actions use credits from your plan. A tailored resume uses one tailoring credit, a cover letter or a LinkedIn or Naukri rewrite uses one writing credit, and a set of interview questions uses one interview credit. LinkedIn and Naukri rewrites, interview preparation and the AI tools inside the Chrome extension need a Pro or Premium plan.

Do the tools share my information?

Yes, within your account. Your Master Profile is the source for resumes, cover letters, ATS checks and profile rewrites, so you add your experience once. Nothing is shared outside your account or published.

Do I need the Chrome extension?

No. Everything except the Naukri profile scan can be done in the Vignova web app by pasting job descriptions. The extension saves time if you apply often: it saves postings from job pages, shows keyword matches and, on Pro and Premium plans, fills supported application forms.

Is Vignova affiliated with LinkedIn or Naukri?

No. Vignova is an independent career tool and is not affiliated with or endorsed by LinkedIn Corporation, Naukri.com or Info Edge (India) Ltd. The LinkedIn and Naukri optimizers help you improve the text you publish on your own profiles.
