Chrome Extension

A Chrome extension that brings your career profile to every job page

Job postings and application forms are spread across hundreds of sites. The Vignova extension works on top of them: save a posting to your tracker, see how its skills compare with your Master Profile and copy your details into forms. On paid plans, you can also tailor your resume or fill the application without switching tabs.

Add to Chrome Create a free account
  • Free to install
  • Works with a free account
  • Chrome 116 or later
On a job page

Everything you need while you browse jobs

Save the job

The extension reads the title, company, location and description from LinkedIn, Indeed and many company career pages. Check the details, then save the job to your tracker. For anything it cannot read, paste the description.

See your keyword score

Compare a posting's skills with your active Master Profile: matched keywords, missing keywords and an overall score. On LinkedIn and Indeed job pages, a match panel shows the score beside the posting.

Add a skill you really have

If the posting names a skill you have but your profile does not list, add it to your Master Profile from the match panel with one click.

Tailor and write

On Pro and Premium, generate a tailored resume, a cover letter, an application email or a message to HR for the posting. Each one is saved with the job, ready to copy or download as a PDF.

Copy your details into forms

The Profile tab keeps your personal details, work experience, education and skills one click from the clipboard, for forms that need typing.

Autofill applications

On Pro and Premium, fill standard application forms from your profile. Autofill is in beta and never clicks Next or Submit.

Where it works

Built for the sites where you look and apply

  • Job pages: LinkedIn and Indeed, with the match panel, and many company career pages that publish their job details in a standard format
  • Application forms: standard forms, including many built on Greenhouse, Lever, Workday, Ashby, SmartRecruiters and iCIMS. Custom forms may need manual input
  • Naukri: your own Naukri profile, read when you start a scan from the Vignova dashboard
  • Any other posting: paste the job description into the extension

Set it up in a few minutes

  1. Add Vignova from the Chrome Web Store and pin it to your toolbar.
  2. Sign in with your Vignova account, or create one for free.
  3. Complete your Master Profile, the source for every score and document.
  4. Open a job posting and choose a tool.
Privacy

What the extension reads, and when

Job postings and application forms live on thousands of different sites, so the extension's scripts load on the pages you visit. They act when you use a tool, with two exceptions: the match panel on LinkedIn and Indeed job pages, and the document picker on file upload fields.

  • A job page is read when you save or analyse it, and its details go to your Vignova account
  • On LinkedIn and Indeed job pages, the posting is sent to Vignova to calculate your keyword score
  • A form is filled only when you start autofill, and it is never submitted for you
  • It never asks for your LinkedIn, Naukri or job site passwords

Read the privacy policy for how Vignova handles your data.

You stay in control of every application. The extension saves, scores, writes and fills. Deciding what to send, and sending it, is always up to you.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Is the Vignova Chrome extension free?

Yes, it is free to install. With a free Vignova account you can save jobs, see keyword scores and copy your profile details. Tailored resumes, cover letters, emails and HR messages need a Pro or Premium plan and use credits, and autofill is available on Pro and Premium.

Which browsers does it work in?

The extension is published on the Chrome Web Store for Google Chrome, version 116 or later.

Does the extension apply to jobs for me?

No. Autofill can fill in an application form, but it never clicks Next, Submit or Apply. You review every answer and submit the application yourself.

What does the extension read on the pages I visit?

It reads a job page when you save or analyse it, and scores postings you open on LinkedIn and Indeed job pages. It fills a form only when you start autofill. When you click a file upload field, it offers the documents in your Vignova account. It never asks for your passwords on job sites.

Does it work with Naukri?

Yes, in two ways. The Naukri profile optimizer uses the extension to read your own Naukri profile when you start a scan from the dashboard. For a Naukri job posting, save it from the posting, or paste its description if the details are not picked up.

Do I need a Vignova account?

Yes. Sign in with your Vignova account so the extension can use your Master Profile and save jobs to your tracker. Creating an account is free.

Why is the keyword score different from my ATS score?

The keyword score compares the skills in a posting with your Master Profile. The ATS resume checker scores one specific resume across six areas: keywords, meaning, sections, impact, format and readability.

Is Vignova affiliated with LinkedIn, Indeed, Naukri or Google?

No. Vignova is an independent career tool. LinkedIn, Indeed, Naukri.com, Google Chrome and the Chrome Web Store are trademarks of their respective owners.
