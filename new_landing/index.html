Can I edit the AI-generated resume?

Yes. Review and edit your draft before exporting. Your Master Profile provides the starting point; you control the final wording.

What does the ATS match score mean?

It is a guide to how well your resume aligns with a job description, including factors such as keyword coverage, content, and structure. It can help you decide what to improve. It is not a prediction of an employer’s screening result or a guarantee of an interview.

Do I need to install the Chrome extension?

No. You can build resumes and use your workspace on the web. The extension adds job saving and application tools to supported pages in Chrome.

Does Vignova apply to jobs for me?

You stay in control of submitting applications. Vignova helps prepare your resume, organize opportunities, and track progress. The Chrome extension connects supported job pages with your workspace.
